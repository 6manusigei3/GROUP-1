# 1. Project Overview

Northstar Retail Co. is a mid-size e-commerce company whose support team is receiving a high volume of repetitive customer questions.

The three main ticket categories are:

1. **Order Status** — "Where is my order?" / "Has this shipped yet?"
2. **Returns & Refunds** — "How do I return this?" / "When will I get my refund?"
3. **Stock Availability** — "Is this back in stock?" / "Do you have this in another size?"

Our team has been engaged for one week to develop a **Support Deflection MVP** that demonstrates how repetitive support tickets can be reduced.

The MVP does not need to be production-ready. It must demonstrate a working approach that can be handed over to Northstar's team.

## MVP Success Criteria

By the end of the sprint, we must deliver:

- A working prototype covering **at least 2 of the 3 ticket categories**.
- An end-to-end demonstration of the prototype.
- A **1-page go-live readiness note**.
- A transparent and traceable collaborative audit trail.
- Project-board tasks with clear ownership, priority and Definition of Done.
- Commit/edit history that can be mapped back to project-board tasks.

## Communication Rules

- Direct project questions should be acknowledged within **2 hours** during agreed working hours where reasonably possible.
- A blocker that prevents progress for more than **30 minutes** should be communicated.
- Important project decisions should not remain only in private messages.
- Decisions affecting scope, architecture, deadlines or responsibilities should be documented.
- Team members should communicate:
  - What they completed.
  - What they are currently working on.
  - What they plan to do next.
  - Any blockers or dependencies.
